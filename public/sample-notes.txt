Solving a linear equation
2x + 6 = 14
Subtract 6 from both sides: 2x = 8
Divide both sides by 2: x = 4
Check: 2(4) + 6 = 14

Try it yourself:
1. 3x + 5 = 20
2. 4x - 8 = 12
Answers: 1. x = 5; 2. x = 5.
